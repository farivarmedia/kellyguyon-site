Profile

As a Strategic Account Executive at Hearst Bay Area, Kelly Guyon works with local and regional businesses across the Bay Area to design multi-channel media campaigns — translating business objectives into media plans with clear conversion goals. She manages accounts across home services, healthcare, legal, retail, and the arts, and serves on the internal AI Champions group, building tools to improve how the sales team works.

Before Hearst, she founded Deathcare Solutions, a consultancy that worked with early-stage funeral homes on licensing, family experience, and CRM implementation, with a focus on water cremation and alkaline hydrolysis.

Earlier still, she joined Tulip Cremation as its founding funeral director and helped it become the largest cremation society in California within a year, overseeing more than 12,000 cremations and building the workflows and training programs that made it run.

She holds a BA from UC Davis and is a licensed funeral director in California. She lives in Oakland.

What is alkaline hydrolysis or water cremation?

Alkaline hydrolysis — also called water cremation or aquamation — is an eco-friendly alternative to flame cremation that uses water and alkaline chemicals to gently reduce a body to bone mineral. It produces a significantly smaller carbon footprint than traditional cremation. Kelly Guyon is a leading consultant in water cremation and alkaline hydrolysis, having advised early-stage funeral homes on implementing the process through her consultancy, Deathcare Solutions.
